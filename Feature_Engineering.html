Feature engineering in the analytics life cycle

It is one important step between data preparation and model evaluation.

1. Business questionWhat decision must we support?
2. Data preparationClean and understand the data.
3. Feature engineeringCreate and select useful signals.
4. Model or analysisFind patterns or make predictions.
5. Decision and monitoringAct, measure, and improve.

Feature engineering does not replace the other stages. It helps connect prepared data to a useful analysis or model.

Feature types describe different parts of customer behavior.

        

          
Dates can show timing and seasonality. A useful customer feature is how long it has been since the last purchase.
DaysSinceLastPurchase = CutoffDate − LastPurchaseDate

          
Group transaction rows by customer to calculate total revenue, order count, and average order value.
df.groupby("CustomerID")["Revenue"].mean()

          
Ratios make values comparable. Interactions combine values when their relationship matters.
DiscountRate = AverageDiscount × 100

          
Region, acquisition channel, and product category may show useful segment differences. A model converts them into machine-readable values.
OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown="ignore")

          
Keep features that are relevant, reliable, and useful on validation data. Remove IDs, duplicates, and fields that cannot be known at prediction time.
Keep: DaysSinceLastPurchase · Remove: CustomerID

          
Data leakage occurs when a feature contains future information. It can make test results look excellent but fail in real use.
Never use FutureOrders to predict future churn.

        

      

    


    

      

        

          
Critical guardrail
Keep the future out of the feature table.
Use information that exists at the decision date. Use future information only to measure what happened later.

One idea to remember
Feature engineering is not just making more columns. It creates useful, time-valid, and decision-ready signals.
Start with the decision. Then build features from information available at that time.

03 / Seaborn and Matplotlib mapping

From visual question to Python function.

Seaborn excels at relational, categorical, distribution, matrix, and pairwise plots. Some composition charts are more straightforward with Matplotlib or pandas' Matplotlib-backed plotting.

Turn a visual into a business insight.

05

Explain the insight

Describe the evidence, caveats, and implications.

Use with care: Circular area, variable-width bar, and 3D area charts appear in some older decision trees but are often harder to interpret than straightforward bar, line, scatter, or area charts. Avoid inferring causation from a scatter plot, and avoid comparing pie slices when precise differences matter.
